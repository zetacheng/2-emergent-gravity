## 0a. Why this task exists, recorded plainly

`P2-RECON-01B-B0` was executed, reviewed and independently verified, and then
was not integrated. Its result has sat on a branch through six subsequent
tasks while `main`'s component baseline remained the earlier assessment.
**Nobody objected; nobody carried it.** The omission originated with the
Researcher, who verified the result and did not follow it with an integration
specification.

This is the standing example behind rule 22's disposition mechanism, and
`O-3` is the obligation that named it. **Landing it does not by itself fix the
gap that let it happen** — three obligations from `P2-PROJ-01-INTEG` still have
no admissible register.
