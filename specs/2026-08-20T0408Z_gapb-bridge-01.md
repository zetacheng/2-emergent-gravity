## 1b. What bounds it — and why the bound is where it is

`L2`'s argument reaches its conclusion **under stated conditions**: an
infrared effective kernel, Symanzik power counting, an improved stress tensor,
up to contact terms. `EXT-01`'s object is described by its own stated
properties: a finite lattice spacing, a stated mass, a finite extent, and no
improvement.

**The question of transfer is therefore, in the first instance, a question
about whether the conditions of the argument are met by the object.** That is
answerable from the manuscript, the repository, and analysis — and it is what
this task does.

**This task runs no new lattice measurement.** It does not compute a
continuum limit, does not vary the spacing, and does not evaluate an improved
operator. If the executor concludes that settling transfer requires such a
measurement, that conclusion is itself a result of this task: it is recorded
under §5's `INDETERMINATE — MEASUREMENT REQUIRED`, with the measurement
specified but **not performed**. A separate task would perform it.

**The bound is placed here** because a bounded derivation that quietly grows
into a multi-spacing lattice campaign has stopped being bounded, and because
whether such a campaign is warranted is a PI decision this task must inform
rather than pre-empt.

## 3. The analysis

    D1   CONDITION BY CONDITION. For each `M1` condition not settled by `M3`,
         determine by analysis whether `EXT-01`'s object satisfies it.
         Record the reasoning, and record separately anything that cannot be
         settled without measurement.

    D2   EACH MISMATCH, CLASSIFIED. Where a condition is not literally met,
         classify the mismatch under §5's (i)-(iv) and record the reasoning.
         **A condition being unmet does not automatically mean the conclusion
         fails; it means the argument as given does not deliver it directly**,
         and the two are different. Where the mismatch is claimed irrelevant
         or controlled, the record states WHY, not merely THAT.

    D3   THE STANDING OF `EXT-01`'S NUMBERS. Record what `EXT-01`'s measured
         values do and do not bear on, given `D1` and `D2`, **without
         re-analysing them**.

         If transfer fails, they are a measurement of a regime the argument
         does not reach. If transfer holds, the record states that the two
         **bear on objects related closely enough for `L2`'s relevant
         conclusion to apply to `EXT-01`'s observable** — **not** that they
         are the same object. `GAP-B` is a transfer question; requiring
         identity of objects would define the bridge away rather than cross
         it.

    D4   NO RECONCILIATION IS TO BE INVENTED. If `D1`–`D3` leave the argument
         and the measurement in apparent conflict, **that is recorded as an
         apparent conflict**, with both sides stated and neither adjusted.
         Proposing a mechanism that would remove the conflict is theory
         construction beyond this task's bound, and `C9` fails on it.

## 5. Pre-registered outcomes

**The question is not whether `EXT-01`'s object literally satisfies `L2`'s
stated assumptions.** It almost certainly does not — the object is
finite-spacing and unimproved by its own description — and a taxonomy resting
on literal satisfaction would return `TRANSFER FAILS` on that observation
alone, which establishes only that `L2` as stated does not *directly* apply.

**The question is whether `L2`'s `O(p⁴/Λ²)` conclusion can be carried to
`EXT-01`'s object.** A mismatch between assumption and object may be

    (i)    absent after measurement — the condition is in fact met;
    (ii)   present but provably irrelevant to the p² conclusion;
    (iii)  present and controlled by an analytic relation that preserves the
           conclusion;
    (iv)   present, and either shown to break the conclusion, or not
           determinable.

    TRANSFER HOLDS
        Every mismatch identified falls under (i), (ii) or (iii), so the
        conclusion is derivably applicable to EXT-01's object.
        **This does not make Q1 FOUND**, and it does not resolve H-EXT-01.
        If it also leaves L2 and EXT-01's numbers in apparent conflict, that
        conflict is recorded per D4 and is not resolved here.

    TRANSFER FAILS
        At least one mismatch is shown to PREVENT the conclusion from applying
        to EXT-01's object — case (iv), first limb. **Not merely that a
        condition is unsatisfied.**
        **This is a completed result and closes GAP-B.** Per the landed
        Resolution path it would mean L2's conclusion does not bear on the
        measured object even with GAP-A closed, and that Q1 would need a
        derivational ground other than L2, or none exists.
        **It is not a finding that the manuscript is wrong.**

    INDETERMINATE — MEASUREMENT REQUIRED
        A mismatch is present and whether its correction affects the
        conclusion cannot be settled without a lattice measurement K4
        forbids. Record which mismatch, and specify the measurement: what is
        varied, what is held, and what result would decide each way.
        **This is a completed result**, and it is the outcome that would
        justify a PI decision on whether to open such a task.

    INDETERMINATE — UNDETERMINED BY READING
        A condition of M1, or the status of a mismatch under (i)–(iv), cannot
        be established from the manuscript or repository and is not settled
        by analysis, and no measurement would settle it either. Record which,
        and what would.

**No outcome is expected or preferred.** `C6` accepts all four. The executor's
report must not describe any outcome as expected, surprising, or
disappointing, and `C10` checks this.
