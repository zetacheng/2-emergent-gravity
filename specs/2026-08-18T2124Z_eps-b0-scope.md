## 0. Why this, and what would change if it worked

**`SRC-01a` landed `FORM DERIVED / SCALE FITTED`.** The chain runs

    SPARC observation → r_c ~ 10 kpc → m_θ ~ 10⁻²⁷ eV → ε

**— observation inward.**

**If `ε` could be computed from the substrate, the chain would run the
other way:**

    microscopic theory → ε → m_θ → r_c

**That would move the verdict from `SCALE FITTED` toward `SCALE
DERIVED`**, and **it would be the programme's first quantitative
prediction at an astronomical scale.**

**This assessment determines whether that is available.** **It does not
attempt it.**

## 2. The four questions

**One: what is the object?** **The manuscript names `ε`'s magnitude on
the `H(4)` substrate, and singles out an anomaly contribution that may
be exponentially instanton-suppressed.** **Report what `ε` is DEFINED
as in this manuscript** — which symmetry it breaks, what breaks it, and
whether the anomaly contribution is the whole of it or one term among
several. **If there are several contributions, name them.**

**Two: what inputs would the computation need, and which are frozen?**
**For each input, report whether it is frozen in `CONVENTIONS.md`, fixed
by the manuscript, or falls in `D-1c`'s `R1`–`R5`** — naming which.

**Report `R1` separately and prominently.** **`ε` measures explicit
breaking, and what breaks a lattice symmetry is a property of the
lattice operator.**

**If `ε` depends on `R1`, this route is BLOCKED PENDING THE SAME OPEN
PROGRAMME RULING that the whole microscopic line waits on** — **and that
is the single most decisive thing this assessment can report.**

**Note the phrasing.** **`R1` is not a ruling that has been made; it is
an OPEN RULING NODE.** **"Blocked by a ruling" would invert what `D-1c`
established** — nothing has been ruled, and the block is a wait, not a
consequence.

**Do not assume it does.** **The Researcher's prior is that it does;
that prior is stated here so you can test it, not adopt it.**

**Three: would `ε` alone fix `m_θ`?** **`m_θ² ~ ε Λ²` carries a `~`.**
**Report what the manuscript says fixes the COEFFICIENT** — a matching
condition, a normalisation, a convention, or nothing. **If nothing does,
say so.**

**A computed `ε` with an unfixed coefficient gives a SCALING LAW, not a
mass.**

**Four: what fixes `Λ`?** **Report what `Λ` is in this manuscript** —
the lattice cutoff, a symmetry-breaking scale, something else — **and
what determines its value.**

**If `Λ` is itself set from observation, the chain does not reverse: it
loops.** **A prediction whose dimensional scale is an observational
input is not a prediction of that scale.**

**Questions three and four together decide whether this route can
produce a NUMBER.** **Report them together as well as separately.**

## 3. The pre-registered verdicts

    TRACTABLE AND NUMERICAL
        ε is computable from frozen inputs, the coefficient is fixed,
        and Λ is determined internally. A number would follow.

    TRACTABLE BUT ONLY A RELATION
        ε is computable, but the coefficient or Λ is unfixed. A scaling
        law would follow, not a mass. NAME WHICH IS UNFIXED.

    BLOCKED PENDING A RULING
        the repository ESTABLISHES that computing ε requires one or
        more presently OPEN R1–R5 programme rulings. NAME WHICH, and
        state separately whether R1 dependence is established.

    NOT DETERMINABLE FROM THIS REPOSITORY
        the repository does not establish enough to determine either the
        computation or its dependency on the open rulings. Name the
        missing evidence.

**The third and fourth are distinguished by WHAT is unestablished.**
**Third: the computation is legible and its dependency on an open node
is established.** **Fourth: either the computation itself or the
dependency mapping is not.**

**A case where computing `ε` is clear but its relation to `R1`–`R5` was
never mapped falls in the FOURTH, not the third** — **dependence not
established is not dependence.**

**All four are legitimate.** **`SRC-01a` established that a derived
RELATION is not a derived VALUE**, and **the second verdict here is the
same distinction applied to a different quantity.** **Do not let a
`TRACTABLE` finding read as `NUMERICAL` if the coefficient or `Λ` is
open.**
