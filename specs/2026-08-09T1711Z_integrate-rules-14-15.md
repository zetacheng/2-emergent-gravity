## 0. What is being integrated

`CONVENTIONS.md`'s execution-discipline rules go from **1–13 to 1–15**,
and four existing rules gain refinements:

    Amendment A  -> Rule 7    mid-task authorizations reproduced
                              verbatim in the task report
    Amendment B  -> Rule 8    every task report carries a
                              "Stops and clarifications" section
    Amendment C  -> Rule 9    digest semantics and binary-safe
                              computation
    Amendment D  -> Rule 13   execution location, and the
                              process/harness layer

    New Rule 14               Validator outcome contract
    New Rule 15               Governing artifacts are committed

**Two provenances, and the source specification kept them apart.**
Amendments A–D and Rule 14 came from a draft approved earlier and never
executed. **Rule 15 was separately PI-approved and had no prior
repository artifact**; the landing specification is the first durable
record of that approval, and says so rather than implying a document
that does not exist.

**Verified independently before this specification was written**, by
extracting each rule section from both revisions: **rules 1–13 keep
their titles, exactly four rule bodies changed — 7, 8, 9 and 13 — and
the other nine are byte-identical.** No rule was silently reworded under
an unchanged heading.

**What this changes for later work, and it is not small.**

**Rule 15 makes specifications, pre-execution reviews, task reports and
supplied manifests committed artifacts.** Pre-execution reviews would be
committed under `reviews/<function>/` **before the work they authorise
proceeds** — which is a change to how the Reviewer function operates,
not only the Executor. **Rule 15 is prospective**: nothing is
back-filled, and the landing task itself was governed by the rules in
force at its evidence base.

**Rule 14 gives validator outcomes a contract**, so that a pass requires
exit 0 and no timeout, and an exception is recorded as a PI-authorized
exception rather than rewritten as a pass.

**What this integration does NOT do:**

- **It does not land Amendments E–L or Rules 16 and 17.** That package
  is separately approved and awaits its own specification. **This
  integration is what makes it landable** — Amendment E attaches to
  Rule 14 — **but it does not land it.**
- **It does not create any `reviews/` record.** Rule 15 is prospective
  and creating one here would be the back-filling it forbids.
- **It does not touch `AGENTS.md`.** Its research rules are a separate
  numbering from `CONVENTIONS.md`'s execution-discipline rules.
- **No gate, no science, no result changes.**
