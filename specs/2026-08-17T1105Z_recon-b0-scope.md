`Z_Proca + k·Z_scalar` k-scan INVALID AS RECOVERED**
(`DECOMP-UNAVAILABLE-AS-RECOVERED`): the recovered `boson_loop` scalar
(`Δ+m²`, propagating) **is not** the flat Proca longitudinal
eigenfactor (`m²`, ultralocal), and cannot be substituted without an

**nothing exists to check a reconstruction against.**

numerical.** **The programme does not know how large it is**, and
`D-pre-B0` established that measuring a job before commissioning it is

## 3. The five questions this assessment answers

**One: is `Δ⁽¹⁾[g,h]` defined anywhere in the repository, or must it be
constructed?** Report where you looked and what you found.

**Two: what does the flat limit have to reproduce, and is that stated
anywhere?** **The Proca eigenvalue structure `{+m²(×3), m²}` is named in
`P2-BETAV-CIRC-01`.** **Report whether a flat-limit validation target is
stated precisely enough to test against**, or whether stating it is part
of the work.

**Three: does the anchor depend on anything not yet frozen? TWO
SEPARATE VERDICTS, and conflating them is the error this question exists
to prevent.**

    3a  does the RATIO (k+2) itself depend on any of R1–R5?
    3b  does the ABSOLUTE or assembled β_V, or the induced-G
        normalisation, depend on any of R1–R5?

**`β_V/β_B = (k+2)` is a species-level determinant-structure RATIO.**
**Assembling every microscopic species multiplicity into a final induced
Newton coefficient is a DIFFERENT quantity.** **`R5` is an OBVIOUS
DEPENDENCY CANDIDATE for `3b`** — **but that is a candidate to test, not
a verdict.** **Both verdicts must be established from repository lines,
and neither is forecast here.**

**An earlier draft asked only whether "the anchor" depends on `R1`–`R5`,
and then reasoned as if a `YES` on `R5` would settle it.** **An executor
following that would answer `YES` because `N` reaches the assembled
quantity — and would misreport a ratio as ruling-dependent when it may
not be.**

**`P2-HK-01` derives the ratio from Seeley–DeWitt `a₁` conventions.**
**Report whether those conventions are frozen in `CONVENTIONS.md`**, and
**give `3a` and `3b` as separate verdicts, each with lines.**

**Four: what would the numerical work actually be?** Report **the
distinct components a reconstruction needs**, and **classify each into
exactly ONE of four MUTUALLY EXCLUSIVE states:**

    1  IMPLEMENTATION + SPECIFICATION
    2  IMPLEMENTATION ONLY
    3  SPECIFICATION ONLY
    4  NEITHER

**and report `N_total = N_both + N_impl + N_spec + N_neither`.**

**An earlier draft offered three overlapping states, so a component
having both would have been countable twice or arbitrarily once.**

**An implementation counts only if it is POTENTIALLY APPLICABLE TO THE
CLEAN-ROOM RECONSTRUCTION.** **A historical recovered implementation may
exist and be unusable here** — **existence is not availability.**

**This classification is SEPARATE from §2's `starting point / check /
neither`.** **Existence state is not clean-room reuse classification**,
and a component may be `IMPLEMENTATION + SPECIFICATION` while being
reusable only as a check.

**Five: what could make it fail, and would the failure be visible?**
**The gate names two kill criteria** — stuck at `3` for all `k` means a
degenerate pipeline; drift toward `5` at heavy mass means a longitudinal
artifact. **Report both, and report whether either could be mistaken for
a physical result.**
