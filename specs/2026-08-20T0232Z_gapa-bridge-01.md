## 3. The derivation

Performed twice, by two independent methods, and both recorded.

    D1   SYMBOLIC. Construct the Barnes–Rivers spin-2 projector at the
         momentum direction of `M2`, in the dimension of `M1`, from the
         definition `M1` records. Determine its rank and a basis for its
         image. Compare that image with the span of `M2`'s five recipes as
         subspaces — not as basis lists, since two different bases may span
         one space.

    D2   NUMERICAL. Independently, construct both subspaces as explicit
         matrices, and compare by a rank or projector-difference test with a
         stated tolerance. Record the tolerance and the observed residual.

    D3   THE SECOND CONJUNCT. Determine whether the complement of the
         Barnes–Rivers TT block, in the index space `M3` establishes, is the
         set `M4` records. **Answer for the set, component by component**, not
         only for the dimension: two five-dimensional spaces need not be the
         same space.

**If `D1` and `D2` disagree, that is a result to report, not a discrepancy to
resolve by preferring one.** Record both and mark the outcome `INDETERMINATE`
under §4.
