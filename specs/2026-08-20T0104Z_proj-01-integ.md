## 1. Objective

Land both branches, and record what each established — **adding nothing to
either.**

## 1a. Rule 17 is the governing constraint of this task

> An integration, derivation, or any task that carries reviewed results
> forward MUST NOT add a governance or epistemic classification the reviewed
> results did not carry.

This task therefore:

1. **does not write `Q1`'s subclass into `PROJ-01`'s artifact.** The subclass
   is a separate reviewed result and lives in its own artifact; the landing
   record cross-references it. **Transporting a reviewed classification is
   permitted; relocating it into a document that did not carry it is not**,
   and would additionally edit an executed artifact;
2. **creates no register entry** — see §6, which registers the obligations
   instead of discharging them;
3. **does not adopt any governance interpretation** offered in review of
   either source. In particular, the interpretation of who may assign a
   subclass to results predating rule 22 is **not** landed here: it is not
   part of either reviewed result, and landing it would be precisely what
   rule 17 forbids. It belongs to the audit specification.

## 1b. Non-objectives

This task does **not**:

1. edit any file arriving from either source branch;
2. re-adjudicate `Q1`, its subclass, or its `Resolution path`;
3. open, scope, or begin either bridge task;
4. integrate `science/recon-01b-b0-scope` — separate, and named in §6;
5. reclassify component 5 or component 9 beyond transporting what the
   reviewed results state;
6. modify any file under `scripts/`;
7. modify any file under `specs/` other than adding this task's own.
