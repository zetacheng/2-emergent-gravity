## 0. What this task produces, and the line it must not cross

This task lands a decoupling prescription for the assembled chain in
the sense `P2-FIERZSUM-01.md:218-220` states: auxiliary variables,
constraints, Jacobian, and an explicit statement of what is generated
dynamically rather than introduced as an independent field.

**The distinction this entire task turns on:**

> The prescription must define the construction and the measure
> sufficiently to UNIQUELY DEFINE the normalization object the landed
> criterion names. It must NOT evaluate it.

The object is `N_α[g]` as it appears in the landed criterion's
identity `Z_HS^(α)[g] = N_α[g] · Z_fermionic[g]`
(`P2-FIERZSUM-01.md:451-460`), of which a Jacobian is at most one
contributor alongside normalization and contour phase, as that
criterion's own wording has it.

**What this task must NOT presuppose.** The landed HS identity
introduces an auxiliary integration variable and does not remove the
fermionic ones. Nothing landed establishes an invertible change of
variables `Dψ Dψ̄ ↔ Dσ`, and this specification does not assert one.
The mathematical form the object takes — functional determinant,
Gaussian normalization factor, contour-dependent phase, or a
combination — is to be IDENTIFIED from the landed construction, not
assumed from this specification's prose. **An artifact that invents a
form in order to have something to define is a defect of this task.**

The separation of concerns is nonetheless as the analogy has it:
fixing a construction can make an object unique without evaluating
it, and asking how that object responds to curvature is a separate
calculation. This task does the first. `P2-XI-QM3-DEP-01`, re-run
under its own later specification, does the second.

**Therefore, and each is an abort:**

- **No curvature-dependence evaluation.** `δ log J_α/δR`,
  `δ log N_α/δR`, or any equivalent, in any form, is A2 — including
  a partial evaluation, an order-of-magnitude remark, or a statement
  of which way it would go.
- **No verdict from the Q-M3 vocabulary.** `DEPENDENT`,
  `INDEPENDENT`, or any equivalent finding about curvature
  dependence. A2.
- **No L-scaling, no magnitude, no sign, no bound** for the Jacobian,
  the normalization, or any factor of either. A2.
- **No resolution of `DET-01` and no choice of `𝔊`.** The measure
  treatment required by the landed construction may be fixed to the
  extent landed authority determines it; the measure itself is landed
  NOT DETERMINABLE and stays so. A3.
- **No membership implication for `Q-M2` or `Q-M3`.** Both remain
  OPEN ledger rows; this task is not the evidence Ruling 1 awaits.
  A2.
- **`OPEN-AC-1` is not closed** and the family-wide
  representation-stability question is not touched. Naming the
  prescription used is not a claim that decouplings are equivalent
  after truncation. A4.
