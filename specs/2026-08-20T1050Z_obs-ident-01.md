## 1. The question

    Q   What is the repository-defined `β_V` observable — a fixed-mass `q²`
        coefficient, or a mass-dependent extraction from that coefficient's
        dependence on mass, or something else — and does `EXT-01`'s
        measurement measure that observable?

**This is a dependency question and it is asked first for that reason.**
`GAP-B`, `MM-1` and `MM-5` concern conclusions drawn about, or transferred to,
`EXT-01`'s object, while `D-2`'s scientific role depends on whether that
object is the target observable or only a proxy. If `EXT-01` measured a
quantity related to but different from the observable the `β_V` line targets,
then the first three would be answering a rigorous question about the wrong
quantity, and the fourth would be a different task from the one deferred.

## 1a. What is NOT being questioned

**`EXT-01` measured what it measured, correctly, and this task does not
revisit its execution.** Its numbers were pre-registered, independently
reproduced, and are not re-analysed here. The question is what they bear on,
not whether they are right.

An outcome of `PROXY ONLY` would mean the measured quantity is related to but
distinct from the target observable. **It would not mean `EXT-01` was
mistaken, void, or wasted**, and `C9` fails on any statement to that effect.

## 1b. Non-objectives

This task does **not**:

1. re-analyse, re-fit, or re-run `EXT-01`'s measurement, or any measurement;
2. run a mass scan, a volume scan, or any new numerical evaluation. `D-2`
   remains deferred and its purpose is one of the things this task may change,
   not something it acts on;
3. attempt `MM-1`, `MM-5` or `MM-3`, or re-open `GAP-A`;
4. conclude anything about whether the discarded external space matters for
   `β_V`. **That follows from this task's outcome and is not part of it**;
5. compute `β_V`, `β_B` or their ratio, or reproduce the frozen anchor's
   value. **Tracing what a quantity is defined to be does not require

6. alter `A-EXT-01`, `H-EXT-01`, or any landed exact statement;
7. modify any file under `scripts/`;
8. merge anything, or move `main`.
