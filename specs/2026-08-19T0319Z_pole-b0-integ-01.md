## 5. Landing record — required content

### 5.1 Two-layer blocker structure

The record MUST carry two separately headed sections. Neither may be folded
into the other, and the record must not anywhere summarise the position as
"blocked pending R1–R4".

    LAYER 1 — RULING BLOCKERS
        components blocked pending an R1–R4 ruling.

    LAYER 2 — CONSTRUCTION BLOCKERS
        components for which no applicable implementation exists and none is
        scoped. These are not cleared by any ruling.

The record must state explicitly that ruling R1–R4 in full would not clear
Layer 2.

### 5.2 Component accounting, transcribed

    12 = 0 both + 3 impl + 3 spec + 6 neither

Transcribed from the branch's own execution report at 3f78acfe. Not
recomputed, not re-derived, not re-partitioned. Mismatch is an A5 abort.

### 5.3 The load-bearing finding

`N_both = 0`: no milestone component holds both an applicable implementation
and a specification. The record states this together with its consequence —
that the construction layer itself has not been established, which is why the
primary verdict is a construction verdict and not a ruling verdict.

### 5.4 Dependency transfer — two kinds, kept distinct

    POLE milestone    inherits R4 UNCONDITIONALLY. Its object is
                      <T_{mu nu}(x) T_{rho sigma}(y)>. The expectation-value
                      bracket enters the DEFINITION of the correlator, not
                      only the operator; without an ensemble / state space /
                      functional measure the correlator is not yet defined.

    SRC-B0            inherits CONDITIONALLY. A Gamma-defined stress tensor
                      inherits; an S-defined one does not; which the programme
                      needs is unresolved.

These two statements must appear separately. Merging them into one
dependency claim loses the finding.

### 5.5 Negative delimitation of existing machinery

The record states each of the following, as delimitations:

1. Barnes–Rivers projector machinery provides channel decomposition. It is not
   an interacting correlator, not pole extraction, and not residue analysis.
2. `tt_check.py` computes four channels and the caller retains the first.
   Recorded as evidence that the presence of building blocks is not the
   presence of a milestone implementation.
3. RECON-01a establishes a curved bosonic determinant / operator construction.
   Metric variations of `logdet` yield an effective-action response, a
   Γ⁽²⁾-class object. It is **not** an interacting stress-tensor correlator and
   is not to be renamed as one.

### 5.6 Verdict as landed

    PRIMARY       REQUIRES A CONSTRUCTION NOT YET SCOPED

    PARTIAL       TRACTABLE BUT BLOCKED PENDING A RULING — recorded as
                  applying to part of the scope only, and explicitly
                  recorded as NOT the primary verdict.

### 5.7 Polarity

Recorded as a negative result, at the same prominence as a positive result
would receive.
