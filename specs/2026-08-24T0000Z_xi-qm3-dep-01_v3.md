## 0. Authority and the single question

`P2-XI-RULINGS-02`, Ruling 2 authorizes this task: perform the undone
check that landed text already names — whether the
Hubbard–Stratonovich Jacobian/normalization term is
curvature-dependent (`P2-FIERZSUM-01.md:451-460`) — and adjudicate
dependence only.

`P2-XI-RULINGS-02-CLARIFICATION-01` scopes it: the check is performed
on **the landed decoupling**, the HS transformation of the assembled
chain. Its verdict is conditional on that representation, and that
conditionality is carried on the artifact alongside its other
conditions.

**The question, and the whole of it:**

> For the landed decoupling α, does `δ log N_α[g] / δR` vanish,
> where `N_α[g]` is the normalization in the identity
> `Z_HS^(α)[g] = N_α[g] · Z_fermionic[g]`?

**The membership consequence is the landed criterion's, not this
task's:** if dependent, the row enters the ledger and its L-scaling
is then to be determined; if independent, the row closes. **This task
states its finding and does not apply the consequence** — membership
is ruled by the PI after this evidence returns (Ruling 1).
