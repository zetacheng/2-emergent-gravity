## 0a. What this task is for

A PI request proposes a line asking what the induced ξ ledger contains when
both species' books are opened. **That request carries a chain of
characterisations, and states that they are chat-level inference rather than
landed fact.**

**This task establishes or refutes them against the repository, before any of
them appears as an assumption in any specification.** It computes nothing and
opens no physics question.

**A characterisation from a prompt is evidence about the prompt, not about the
object.** This principle is landed in this repository and was arrived at by
measurement: a property named in a frozen pre-registration was later found to
appear nowhere in the artifact it characterised.

## 0c. Claim 5 is a different kind of claim, and is treated differently

Claims 1 to 4 are about the physics chain and are answerable by reading what
the repository states.

**Claim 5 is a meta-claim about the proposed test.** It is also a universal
negative — *no* free parameter exists — and **a universal negative is not
established by failing to find a counterexample in an unbounded search.** This
repository has a catalogued defect class of exactly that shape.

**This task therefore does not attempt to verify Claim 5.** It determines one
thing about it:

    Q5   Can the upstream of the ξ ledger be delimited, from landed state,
         into a bounded set of choice points over which a census could run?

    ANSWERABLE       the boundary exists in landed state. Record it, with
                     the evidence, and record what would be enumerated.
                     **The census itself is a separate task**, `XI-B0b`.
    NOT ANSWERABLE   no landed statement delimits the ledger's upstream.
                     **This is a result**: Claim 5 cannot be given a bounded
                     form until something else fixes the boundary.

**Q5's answer depends on Claim 4**, since the ledger's boundary is what
"clean regime" would fix. If Claim 4 is refuted or undetermined, `Q5` may be
`NOT ANSWERABLE` for that reason, and **that is a finding, not a failure.**

## 2. What counts as landed, stated before the search

A statement is **landed** if it is on `main` at `§0`'s base.

**Being on `main` does not make every statement the same kind of evidence**,
and `M1` records the kind. The repository has a landed ruling that a
specification transcribing an adjudication is evidence of content but not that
adjudication's canonical record; **the analogous distinction for a physics
result — between a derivation, a gate statement, and a session log — is
recorded by `M1` and not decided here.**

**This task does not rank those kinds.** It records what carries what, so that
a later task, or the PI, can decide what weight each bears.

## 3. Abort conditions

    A1   the base SHA observed differs from §0
    A2   `main` moves during execution
    A3   the landed `SI-2` scope statement cannot be located
    A4   a model-level question must be answered to classify a claim or to
         settle `M4`. **The executor does not decide it.** The scope of the
         stop is the smallest unit that contains the question:

             a claim          stop that claim's submeasurement, record the
                              model-level question, classify that claim
                              `UNDETERMINED BY READING`, and continue the
                              remaining claims
             `M4`             record the question and answer `Q5`
                              `NOT ANSWERABLE` per §0c, giving the question
                              as the reason

         **Every claim therefore receives a classification and `Q5` receives
         an answer**, so `C2`, `C3` and `C5` remain satisfiable whenever `A4`
         fires.
    A5   `M5` establishes overlap with `SI-2`. **Complete the remaining
         measurements, then stop before proposing any ordering or any
         follow-on task**, and return the overlap for a PI ruling. **The
         assessment is still produced**; what stops is the proposal of what
         comes next.
    A6   a path outside the §0e manifest is modified

**`A1`, `A2`, `A3` and `A6` stop execution. `A4` and `A5` stop something
narrower and say what** — `A4` a single claim's submeasurement or `M4`, `A5`
the proposing of follow-on work. **Neither is covered by any acceptance
criterion**, and the narrowing is stated here rather than in a criterion so
that none contends with them. **`A4` leaves every criterion satisfiable**,
because the unit it stops still yields a recorded value.
