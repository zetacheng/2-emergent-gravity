## 0a. The question, and why it is asked of this object

`P2-OBS-IDENT-01` established that the species coefficient is the `m² ln m²`
coefficient of `Z(m²)`, and that `EXT-01` measured `Z` at a single mass — the
input to that extraction, not the extraction.

**This task performs the extraction, per external component.**

    Q   Do the discarded external components carry `m² ln m²` content, and at
        what size relative to the retained ones?

**Why this object rather than a transferred theorem.** `GAP-B` asked whether a
suppression argument derived for one object carries to another, and returned
`INDETERMINATE` on mismatches including species. **This task needs no
transfer**: it measures the mass-log content of the object itself. `MM-1`,
`MM-3` and `MM-5` are not engaged, and this task neither answers nor disturbs
them.

## 0b. What each outcome would and would not mean — stated before measuring

    DISCARDED LOG CONTENT SMALL RELATIVE TO RETAINED
        The scheme-independent content would sit predominantly in the
        retained space. **This does not establish `H-EXT-01`**; it is
        evidence bearing on it, over one object at one lattice size.

    DISCARDED LOG CONTENT COMPARABLE TO RETAINED
        The retained space would not carry the scheme-independent content
        alone. **This does not change `β_V`'s value** — `β_V` is by
        definition the log coefficient of the retained space. It would mean
        `β_V` is not the whole of the scheme-independent story.
        **Reach, not correctness**, and the record must say so.

**Neither outcome is expected or preferred.** `C9` checks the report
characterises none as expected, surprising or disappointing.

## 0c. There is no statistical uncertainty here, and none is manufactured

The lattice sum is deterministic: the same inputs return the same number.
**No sampling or statistical uncertainty is present**, so a bootstrap or a
residual read as a probabilistic error bar would describe a process that does
not occur.

**Residuals are not discarded, and they are not error bars.** A fit residual
may diagnose fit quality or model mismatch — that a component's `Z(m²)` is
poorly described by the frozen form — and is recorded for that purpose under
`E3`. **It is not interpreted as an uncertainty on `β_i`.**

**The reported band is solely the frozen variant sensitivity spread.**

**The only meaningful band is the spread across a frozen variant set** — the
range a quantity takes over the analysis choices this specification fixes.
It measures sensitivity to known choices, not dispersion under repetition.

**The variant set is frozen in §2 and is not enlarged or reduced during
execution.** The band *is* its spread, so changing the set changes the band.
`A5` and `C6` enforce this.

## 1. The frozen protocol — fixed before any number exists

**Every element below is committed before the commit that produces a result.**
`E1` states the mechanism and `C3` verifies it by commit order, not by
assertion. This is the method `EXT-01` used and which was independently
verified.

### 1.1 The object and its decomposition

The external components `EXT-01` enumerates, in the same basis, at the same
momentum direction, read from the landed artifact — **not restated here, and
not counted here**. Their number, identities and basis are `M1`'s to read.

### 1.2 The two-step extraction

    step 1   at fixed mass, vary q; the q² coefficient of the component's
             Π(q) is that component's Z(m²)
    step 2   vary mass; the m² ln m² coefficient of Z(m²) is that
             component's β

**Step 1 is what `EXT-01` performed at one mass.** This task performs it per
mass and then performs step 2.

### 1.3 Parameters, frozen

    lattice extent      n = 12, as EXT-01 used
    q grid              the grid the landed pipeline uses by default, read
                        under M1. **Its size is not stated here.**
    mass windows        BOTH of the windows the landed pipeline defines,
                        each used in full. Neither is primary.
    fit form            inherited from the landed pipeline, NOT chosen here

**`n = 12` carries a recorded limitation.** At the light window's lightest
point the product of mass and extent is small, and finite-volume contamination
is worst there; at the heavy window the longitudinal mode's flat-band
behaviour dominates. **Both are target-free objections and neither window is
free of one**, which is why both are used and neither is primary. The figures
are read under `M1`, not restated here.

### 1.4 The assembly rule, frozen — retained and discarded are not symmetric in provenance

**The retained aggregate has a landed definition; the discarded aggregate does
not.** This asymmetry is real and is recorded rather than smoothed.

    RETAINED    The landed pipeline assembles the axis-TT slope from the
                retained components with weights `w_i`. **`M1` reads `w_i`
                from the landed pipeline and shows the line that fixes them.**
                They are NOT assumed to be unity, and this specification does
                not state them.

                **A weight of the form `1/N_R` and a weight of `1` differ by
                a factor of `N_R` in every ratio reported here.** `M1`
                establishing which is landed is therefore load-bearing, and
                `A8` fires if it cannot.

    DISCARDED   The repository has never assembled these components, so no
                landed weight exists. **`v_i` is therefore a choice of this
                task, and is labelled as one wherever a result uses it.**

                `v_i` is fixed to the same functional form as `w_i` — if the
                landed retained weights are an average, `v_i` is an average
                over the discarded set; if a plain sum, a plain sum. **The
                two sides are made structurally alike so the ratio compares
                like with like**, and the choice is recorded as this task's,
                not as a landed fact.

The two aggregates and the two ratios, fixed here and committed under `M2`:

    B_R  =  Σ_{i∈R}  w_i β_i
    B_D  =  Σ_{i∈D}  v_i β_i

    R_signed  =  |B_D| / |B_R|
    R_abs     =  ( Σ_{i∈D} |v_i β_i| ) / ( Σ_{i∈R} |w_i β_i| )

**Both are reported always**, per §1.6. `R_signed` is sensitive to
cancellation between components and `R_abs` is not; the landed `EXT-01`
figures already differ between these two forms at the `Z` level, which is why
neither stands alone.

**If `M1` finds the landed assembly is not of the form `Σ w_i β_i`** — a
projector normalisation, a multiplicity factor, or any other structure — then
`B_R` is defined as the landed assembly states it, `v_i` follows that form,
and the formulas above are replaced accordingly **in the `M2` commit, before
any result exists**. `A8` covers the case where the landed form cannot be
determined at all.

### 1.5 The frozen variant set

The band is the spread over exactly:

    V1   mass window        the two windows of §1.3
    V2   fit form           the inherited form, and one sensitivity form
                            differing from it in a single stated respect

**Both dimensions are frozen now.** `V2`'s sensitivity form is named in the
artifact **before any result is produced**, under `E1`. **No variant is added
after a number exists, and none is dropped.**

### 1.6 What is reported

    PRIMARY      per component, β_i, over every variant combination
    PRIMARY      the ratio of discarded to retained log content, in BOTH
                 aggregations: the magnitude of the sum, and the sum of the
                 magnitudes. **Both, always.** They can differ by a factor
                 the landed EXT-01 numbers already exhibit at the Z level.
    BAND         the spread of each reported quantity over the §1.5 set
    SECONDARY    the absolute spread of the retained aggregate on its own

**The secondary quantity exists to make the primary readable.** If the
retained aggregate is itself poorly resolved across variants, the ratio can be
spuriously stable — numerator and denominator moving together. **Reporting the
ratio's band without the denominator's own band would hide that.**

    PREDICTION   the ratio's spread is expected SMALLER than the spread of
                 its numerator and denominator separately, because both are
                 produced by one pipeline on one grid and shared systematics
                 partly cancel.

**This prediction is recorded so that it can fail.** If the ratio's spread is
not smaller, the cancellation is not present, and that is a result about the
method — recorded, not explained away.

### 1.7 No criterion

**No threshold, pass band, or acceptance range is set on any physical
quantity.** The task reports the ratio with its band and stops. **A
criterion set after seeing the values would be post-hoc**, and no target-free
ground for one presently exists.

`C8` fails on any criterion, verdict, or statement that a value is acceptable,
small enough, or large enough.

## 3. Execution rules

    E1   **Pre-registration precedes results.** `M2`'s commit contains no
         value produced by `M4`–`M6`. `C3` verifies by commit order against
         the committed blob, not against the working tree.

    E2   **The variant set is not modified after `M2`.** If execution shows a
         variant to be unrunnable, that is recorded as a property of the
         frozen set and the variant is reported as `NOT EVALUABLE` with the
         reason — **it is not replaced.**

    E3   **No value is discarded, and numerical validity is decided by rule,
         not by inspection.** The rule is frozen here and committed under
         `M2`:

             solver              the solver the landed pipeline uses, read
                                 under `M1` and not chosen here
             NON-FINITE          any NaN or infinity in a coefficient
                                 -> that fit FAILS
             EXACTLY SINGULAR    a design matrix the solver reports as rank
                                 deficient -> that fit FAILS
             HIGH CONDITION      a finite, full-rank fit whose condition
                                 number exceeds no threshold set here
                                 -> the value IS reported, **flagged with its
                                 condition number**. **No condition number is
                                 a pass/fail line**, consistent with §1.7.
             RESIDUAL            reported per fit, as a diagnostic of fit
                                 quality or model mismatch per §0c.
                                 **Not an error bar, and not a fail
                                 criterion.**

         A component that FAILS under one variant but not another is reported
         `NOT EVALUABLE` **for that variant only**, with which rule fired.
         `A4` concerns a component that FAILS under every frozen variant.

    E4   **No aggregation is chosen.** Both are computed and both reported,
         per §1.6.

    E5   **Deterministic reproduction is checked.** One component's `β` is
         recomputed in a second independent pass and compared byte-for-byte
         at the printed precision. A difference is a defect in the
         computation, not noise.

`A5` is a set that was changed, and `A4` is a component with no value under
any variant.
