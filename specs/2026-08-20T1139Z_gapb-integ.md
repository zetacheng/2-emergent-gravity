## 0a. Why this lands now rather than after a re-scope

`P2-OBS-IDENT-01` established that `EXT-01` measured an input to the target
coefficient rather than the coefficient itself, and `R-2` registers that the
scope of `GAP-B` and its mismatches is undecided in consequence.

**Waiting for that adjudication before landing would repeat a failure this
line has already made.** `P2-RECON-01B-B0` sat executed, reviewed and verified
on a branch through several subsequent landings because its landing had no
owner. **The count is deliberately not stated**: the figure this line carried
for it was asserted, and when measured came back as neither the asserted value
nor a single value — which is part of why §6 exists.
`GAP-B` has been on a branch since before three landings.

**Landing is therefore separated from re-scoping.** This task transports the
finding as it stands. `R-2` remains open, and §5.4 records that the finding is
to be read subject to it — **which is not the same as re-scoping it here**,
and rule 17 forbids the latter.

## 6. One re-measurement, and why an integration performs it

**A figure this line has repeated was not measured.** Three preceding
landings enumerated a set of registers, reported obligations homeless on that
enumeration, and did not read `DECISION_LOG.md`. **How many registers each
enumerated, and how many obligations were reported homeless, are read under
`M10` rather than stated here** — restating either would repeat the defect.

`P2-OBS-IDENT-INTEG` then found that register admits records of that shape on
its own stated scope and its own precedent.

    M10   For each obligation currently recorded as having no admissible
          register — enumerate them by reading, do not carry a count —
          determine against `DECISION_LOG.md`'s STATED SCOPE whether it is
          admitted. Record per obligation: admitted, not admitted, or not
          determinable from the stated scope.

**This task does not place them.** Placement is a separate action with its own
authority question. `M10` establishes whether the standing claim is true, and
`C9` requires the result recorded whichever way it falls.

**Why here rather than in a dedicated task**: the claim is this line's own
repeated assertion, it is measurable by reading, and leaving it standing while
landing another artifact that could inherit it would propagate it a fourth
time. **This is a measurement, not a placement**, and §1's prohibitions are
unaffected.
