## 0. Authority and what this task produces

`P2-XI-RULINGS-02`, Ruling 3 authorizes this task and states its
content. This specification does not restate the ruling's directive
in its own words; the Executor extracts it verbatim at M1 and the
artifact quotes it.

**What this task produces is an enumeration with a classification.**
For a bounding computation of the condensate scalar's own fluctuation
loop, it enumerates the inputs such a computation requires, and
classifies each under the four-outcome vocabulary M3 fixes:
`LANDED` (including `LANDED — NOT DETERMINABLE`), `DERIVABLE`,
`REQUIRING A PI RULING`, and
`ROUTED TO PI — CLASSIFICATION NOT DETERMINABLE`.
That is the whole deliverable.

**The bounding computation is a separate later task and is not
authorized by the ruling.** Nothing here performs it, designs it,
scopes its method, orders it, or estimates any quantity it would
produce.

**Precedent for the artifact's discipline:** `P2-XI-B0a`, the scope
assessment of the induced ξ ledger, records at its `§5` that *this
artifact proposes nothing*, and at its `§6` that *every question
below is recorded, none is answered here, and the fact that a
question is listed is not evidence about its answer*. This task
adopts both sentences as binding constraints on its own artifact, and
M5 requires them to appear in it.

## 0a. Prohibitions, each from a named authority

- **No computation and no estimate.** No value, bound, sign, order of
  magnitude, or L-scaling for the condensate scalar's loop or for any
  input. Ruling 3 reserves the bounding computation to a later task.
  (A2)
- **No membership disposition.** `Q-M2` remains an OPEN ledger row;
  `P2-XI-RULINGS-02` Ruling 1 defers membership until evidence
  returns. This task returns scope, not membership. (A2)
- **No resolution of DET-01.** The ruling directs that its status be
  STATED and not resolved. No choice of `𝔊`. (A3)
- **No ledger edit, no gate movement, no PASS/FAIL.** (A2)
- **No ordering claim.** Ruling 4 makes the Q-M3 check and this task
  mutually non-blocking; this artifact states no ordering between
  them and no priority for the later computation. (A2)

## 1. Conditions carried on the artifact

    COND-D  Functional-measure status. DET-01 is landed NOT
            DETERMINABLE. Wherever an input depends on the functional
            measure, the artifact states that status and assigns the
            input its classification on that basis; it does not
            resolve the measure and does not treat the dependence as
            an obstacle to classification.
    COND-S  STATUS, NOT VALUE. Classification applies to the
            REPOSITORY STATUS of a required input, never to the
            possession of its value. Where landed text fixes that an
            input is NOT DETERMINABLE under DET-01, the status is
            landed and is classified `LANDED — NOT DETERMINABLE`; the
            unknown value is NOT thereby classified as obtained, and
            the input is not routed to the PI on that ground alone
            unless the parent ruling separately directs a PI choice.
            An artifact sentence that reads as though a value were in
            hand because its status is landed is a defect of this
            task.
    COND-E  Enumeration is not exhaustion. The artifact states, in
            these terms, that its input list is what this assessment
            found and not a proof that no further input exists; and
            that a later task encountering an unlisted input is not
            thereby in conflict with this one.

    A1  A node named in M1 cannot be located at the Base. STOP;

    A2  Any step would compute, estimate, bound, sign, or order any

        report.
    A3  Deciding which outcome applies to an input would itself
        require the model-level choice at issue, or would require
        resolving DET-01 or choosing `𝔊`. This is NOT a task failure
        and NOT an unclassified input: assign the fourth outcome
        `ROUTED TO PI — CLASSIFICATION NOT DETERMINABLE`, record it
        under M4, and CONTINUE with the remaining inputs. Note that
        an input whose landed status is NOT DETERMINABLE is NOT this
        case — that is `LANDED — NOT DETERMINABLE` under COND-S. The
        task stops only if every enumerated input carries the fourth
        outcome, in which case the artifact records that as its
        finding under Rule 22 with its subclass and a symmetric
        resolution path the Executor defines and does not walk.
    A4  Any step would perform, design, method-scope, or authorize
        the bounding computation itself. STOP; the ruling reserves it
        to a separate later task.
