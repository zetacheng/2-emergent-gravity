## 0. What this task is and is not

**This task transports one executed, reviewed result to `main` and
does nothing else.** Under Rule 17 it adds no classification the
reviewed result did not carry.

The arriving result is the clarification landing, executed under v2,
stopped at M4 under A3, and resumed and completed under v3 after the
PI ruled the applicable registration mechanism. It carries: the
issued clarification byte-exact, its document review, the register
record with its appended overtaken-statement note, the canonical
record of the PI register-routing ruling, the open-item registration
and landing index in `DECISION_LOG.md`, both specs, all three review
artifacts, and both reports (the stop report and the resumption
report).

- **The registered open item arrives as registered, not authorized.**
  Its status is `UNESTABLISHED. REGISTERED, NOT AUTHORIZED.` and its
  escalation condition is recorded and untriggered. This task does
  not authorize, schedule, prioritise, or trigger it, and does not
  restate its status in any other words.
- **The PI ruling arrives with its own scope limits.** It settles the
  routing of one item, extends no register's scope, creates no
  register, and makes no general decision for future XI-line items.
  This task does not generalise it.
- The two OPEN ledger rows remain OPEN; no membership is disposed.
- It does not begin, schedule, or constrain the Q-M3 check or the
  Q-M2 scope assessment. **Satisfying Q-M3's M0 precondition is a
  consequence of this transport, not a purpose this task may act on.**
- It does not move the source branch or any ref beyond the two this
  spec authorizes (the integration branch and main).
