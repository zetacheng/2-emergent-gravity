## 0. Authority and scope

This task executes the first step of the XI line under the landed PI
rulings (`decisions/P2-XI-RULINGS-01.issued.md`):

- **Ruling 1**: the death-verdict object is `ξ(G)`, the total. This
  task assembles a CONDITIONAL `ξ(G)` — conditional in the sense of
  §1 below — and is not a verdict of any kind.
- **Ruling 2**: the ledger decomposes by curvature-coupling
  structure. The ledger constructed here uses that axis.
- **Ruling 3**: the analytic route — assembly from the `P2-HK-01`
  heat-kernel coefficients — is first priority, inheriting no part of
  the `EXTCOMP-01` fit instability. The ruling's own STOP clause is
  A1 of this specification, verbatim in force: if the analytic route
  is shown unviable at specification or execution stage, STOP;
  ordering returns to the PI; no automatic fallback to the lattice
  route.

**Phase structure — this specification's protocol design, subject to
this review:** Phase 1 uses landed membership only and does not
answer `Q-M2` or `Q-M3`. The verdict-grade reading is Phase 2, which
does not exist until the PI rules on membership — that sequencing is
not new authority; it restates the landed ROUTING clause (model-level
questions go to the PI). Approval of this specification approves the
phase design as protocol; no PI ruling is transcribed or implied by
it.

This task does not adjudicate `H-XI-SIGN-01`. The per-class table it
produces (M3) is data; the hypothesis's establishment or refutation
requires the later reviewed specification its own entry names
(`assumptions/H-XI-SIGN-01.md`), which must operationally define
"coupling structure" and pre-register its discriminating test. No
sentence of this task's artifacts states or implies a status change
for `H-XI-SIGN-01`.

## 1a. Pre-registered L range and representative points, frozen here

The landed texts state the regime qualitatively (`L ≫ 1`, and the
recorded survival boundaries) but fix no canonical finite grid. The
grid is therefore pre-registered HERE, before any evaluation, as a
sampling choice and nothing else:

    Range                L ∈ [0.5, 20]
    Representative pts   L ∈ {0.5, 1, 2, 3, 5, 10, 20}

Rationale for the span, stated so the grid is inspectable and fixed:
the landed survival-window boundaries m > 0.368Λ and m > 0.287Λ
(P2-NORM-01:83) correspond under L = ln(Λ²/m²) to L ≈ 2.00 and
L ≈ 2.50 respectively — a derived correspondence M4 must reproduce,
not an input — so the grid spans from below that neighbourhood
(L = 0.5) through it (L = 2, 3) into the landed `L ≫ 1` regime
(L = 10, 20). M4 evaluates this range and these points and no
others; M1 additionally extracts, verbatim, any landed L-regime or
domain statement bearing on this discussion, for the record — an
extracted statement does not alter the frozen grid within this
task; if one contradicts the grid's suitability, that is A3.

MARGIN NORMALIZATION, frozen here before any evaluation: the margin
is reported as

    r_margin(L) = |δξ_flip(L)| / F0,    F0 ≡ |4Gβ_F|

in each convention (F0 carries the convention of its 4Gβ_F). F0 is
the landed fermionic COEFFICIENT scale, deliberately L-independent:
the alternative normalization by the full L-dependent fermion
contribution |4Gβ_F(3−L)| vanishes at the pre-registered point
L = 3 and is NOT used; no other denominator may be substituted at
execution time. The absolute |δξ_flip(L)| is reported alongside
r_margin at every point, so the reader can form any other ratio
after the fact without the task having chosen one on the evidence.
