### `S-2` — the audit rule earned its place twice

§2's rule — a characterisation quoted from a prior artifact is evidence about
the artifact, not about the object — was applied literally, and it changed two
things.

**First, `M4` was confirmed against the code and not only against the landed
artifact.** The artifact says the mass was pre-registered at a single value;
the script shows it as a module constant that never varies and shows that

inherited characterisation had entered a frozen pre-registration unchecked. **It
