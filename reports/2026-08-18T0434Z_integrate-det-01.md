## 7. `A6` — the rider re-derived, not transcribed

### 7.1 `det[√g g⁻¹] = det g` in four dimensions — SYMBOLICALLY

**Method stated: SYMBOLIC, with `sympy` 1.14.0**, over a general symmetric `4×4`
matrix `g` with ten independent symbols — not a numerical spot check.

    g            a general symmetric 4x4 matrix of ten free symbols
    G1           sqrt(det g) * g.inv()
    simplify( det(G1) - det(g) )        0
    identity holds symbolically         True

**And the general-`d` statement, which is why four is the special case:**

    det[√g g⁻¹] = (det g)^{d/2} / det g = (det g)^{d/2 − 1}
    at d = 4:  (det g)^1 = det g

**In `d ≠ 4` the ambiguity would not reduce to `det g`** — it would still be
ultralocal, which is all the rider needs, but the clean equality is a
four-dimensional fact.

### 7.2 `det G1` is a product over sites, and carries no `m` — NUMERICALLY, against the frozen code

**Method stated: NUMERICAL, run against the frozen `scripts/recon2026/proca_curved.py`
at blob `03f46905…`, on a `4⁴` lattice with a non-flat cosine background
(`amplitude 0.03`, a symmetric non-diagonal `c_{μν}`, wavevector `(0,0,1,0)`).**

    largest off-site entry of G1                    0.0            EXACTLY zero
    log det G1, full 1024x1024 matrix               -0.10775238428919313
    sum over sites of log det of the 4x4 block      -0.10775238428919343
    difference                                      2.9e-16
    max over sites |det G1_block(x) - det g(x)|     1.1e-16

**`G1` has no mass argument in its signature and is bit-identical across three
