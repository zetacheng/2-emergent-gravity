### 5d. What the margin is not

**`|δξ_flip(L)|` is a property of the assembled function.** It answers "how
large would an added term have to be". **It is NOT a statement that the `Q-M2`
or `Q-M3` terms have that size, that sign, or any size or sign at all.** The
artifact says so at the point of use, and this task offers no estimate, bound,
or sign guess for either.
