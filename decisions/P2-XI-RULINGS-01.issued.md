## RULING 1 — The observable the survival condition applies to (Q5)

RULING      The death-verdict object is ξ(G), the total.

RULING      Revision condition: if a registered result establishes ξ(0)
            as non-physical (a scheme artifact), this ruling is open to
            re-adjudication toward Δξ(G). Until then, nothing in this
            ruling asserts the physicality of ξ(0); the ruling states
            only that the death verdict attaches to the total.

RATIONALE   The survival condition concerns whether the condensate
            exists as configured; existence is a total-state question.
            The landed text's own statement — that ξ(G) is "the total
            that determines the gravitational sign" — is what this
            ruling follows.

RULING      The tension noted at issuance — the baseline-subtraction
            deliverable marked primary — is read by the PI as primary
            for the diagnostic decomposition, not primary as the
            death-verdict object. If this reading conflicts with landed
            text, report the conflict for ruling; do not reconcile it
            inside a specification.

## RULING 2 — The decomposition axis of the ξ ledger

RULING      The ledger decomposes by curvature-coupling structure.
            "Per-channel composition" in this line refers to
            coupling-structure entries. Where existing documents use
            "channel" in a conflicting sense, this ruling governs
            forward terminology, or the wording is to be changed.
            Frozen or historical reviewed records are not rewritten
            merely to conform; where necessary, the repository's
            clarification, erratum, or supersession mechanism applies.
            Surface any case where neither course is possible.

RULING      Separation confirmed: the physical proposition that sign is
            driven by coupling structure rather than species is NOT
            adjudicated here and cannot be established or refuted by PI
            ruling. It is to be registered as an H-type hypothesis,
            status UNESTABLISHED, with establishment or refutation
            proceeding through the normal reviewed specification and
            evidence path. This ruling fixes ledger language only.

RATIONALE   An axis with demonstrated explanatory weakness (species:
            the minimal scalar and the fermion share a sign) would
            require annotation on every ledger line. The
            coupling-structure axis lets the ledger and the hypothesis
            it will test speak one language.

## RULING 3 — SI-2 ordering

RULING      Option (a): the XI line proceeds first and produces inputs
            SI-2 will consume.

RULING      Condition: the XI line takes the analytic route — assembly
            from the P2-HK-01 heat-kernel coefficients — as first
            priority, inheriting no part of the EXTCOMP-01 fit
            instability. If the analytic route is shown unviable at
            specification or execution stage (for example, if assembly
            requires lattice-only input), STOP; ordering returns to the
            PI for re-ruling. Do not fall back to the lattice route
            automatically.

RATIONALE   The PI's objective is the earliest credible life-or-death
            reading. If viable, the analytic ledger is itself that
            first reading, and SI-2 then convenes with it in hand; if
            not viable, the cost comparison between orderings changes
            and must be re-made.
