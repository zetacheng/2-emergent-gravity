## 1. Review of the revision — PASS

This revision resolves the principal defect identified in the previous review.

The task no longer defines successful transfer as requiring EXT-01's finite-spacing unimproved object to literally satisfy every assumption appearing in the manuscript argument.

Instead, it asks the scientifically relevant question: whether the manuscript's suppression conclusion is derivably applicable to the EXT-01 observable despite any object, regime, improvement, or contact-term mismatch.

That is the correct formulation of GAP-B.

## 2. Transfer semantics — PASS

`TRANSFER HOLDS` must mean that every relevant mismatch is either absent, proven irrelevant to the target p² conclusion, or controlled by a derivable relation that preserves that conclusion.

This correctly distinguishes literal identity of assumptions from justified transfer of a conclusion.

## 3. Failure semantics — PASS

`TRANSFER FAILS` requires more than finding that one manuscript assumption is not literally satisfied.

A failure requires a demonstrated obstruction: at least one mismatch must be shown to prevent the manuscript conclusion from applying to EXT-01's observable.

This prevents a conservative but logically invalid conversion of “not directly covered” into “transfer disproved”.

## 4. Indeterminate outcome — PASS

Where a mismatch exists but the repository cannot establish whether its correction changes the relevant p² conclusion, the correct result is `INDETERMINATE`.

If resolving that question requires a new continuum extrapolation, improvement study, multi-spacing computation, or other new measurement outside this task, the report should identify that requirement rather than manufacture a conclusion.

## 5. Object-relation formulation — PASS

The revised formulation correctly avoids requiring the manuscript and EXT-01 to concern literally the same object.

The relevant requirement is that the objects be related strongly enough, by a demonstrated analytic argument, for the manuscript's relevant conclusion to apply to EXT-01's observable.

This is essential because GAP-B is itself an object/regime transfer problem.

## 6. Order of reasoning — PASS

The specification correctly requires the transfer question to be settled before comparing the manuscript conclusion with EXT-01's measured discarded contribution.

The approximately 30% discarded/retained result must not be used to motivate, tune, or reverse-engineer the bridge.

The proper order is:

`derive applicability -> classify transfer -> compare consequences`.

## 7. Symmetric pre-registration — PASS

A positive result, a demonstrated negative result, and an unresolved result are all admissible outcomes.
