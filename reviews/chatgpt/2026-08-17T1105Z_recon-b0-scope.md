## Review scope

This review is bound to the exact uploaded specification bytes identified by the SHA-256 above.

The revised RECON-B0 specification was reviewed for clean-room scope, repository inventory requirements, treatment of historical/recovered code, curved-space operator requirements, flat-limit regression targets, separation of the `(k+2)` ratio anchor from absolute/assembled beta_V and induced-G normalization, component-state accounting, kill-criterion extraction, prohibition on reconstruction computation, and interaction with open D-pre ruling nodes R1–R5.

## 1. Assessment-only boundary — PASS

RECON-B0 remains an assessment task. It must build nothing and compute no new reconstruction physics output. Existing scripts may be read as evidence but are not to be executed as reconstruction machinery.

## 2. Existing-script and recovered-code treatment — PASS

The identified scripts must be inspected individually and classified by clean-room role (`starting point`, `check`, or `neither`) independently of whether implementation/specification material merely exists.

`recovered_2026/proca_loop.py` receives explicit scrutiny without being promoted to authoritative clean-room implementation or used to rehabilitate the quarantined historical Finding 5 result.

## 3. Curved-space operator and flat-limit target — PASS

The task asks whether the repository already contains a sufficiently explicit metric-coupled first-order operator variation, including the required `Delta^(1)[g,h]`-type object, without deriving it during B0.

It separately asks whether the flat-limit Proca spectrum is stated precisely enough to serve as a later regression target.

## 4. Ratio anchor versus absolute normalization — PASS

A8 correctly requires two independent verdicts:

- A8a: whether `beta_V / beta_B = k + 2` itself depends on R1–R5;
- A8b: whether absolute/assembled `beta_V` or induced-G normalization depends on R1–R5.

The specification explicitly prevents a dependency of the assembled quantity from being projected back onto the species-level ratio.

## 5. Revised A8 anti-bias wording — PASS

The revised text no longer forecasts A8b's answer.

R5 is identified only as an **obvious dependency candidate** for A8b, expressly described as a candidate to test rather than a verdict. The specification then requires both A8a and A8b to be established from repository lines and states that neither verdict is forecast.

Rule 16 likewise says that A8b **may return a dependency and may not**.

This removes the previous residual tension between independent assessment and answer-suggesting framing.

## 6. Parallel-versus-downstream rule — PASS

Parallel-versus-serial status is determined by A8a alone.

A dependency of final microscopic assembly or induced-G normalization does not by itself force the clean-room `(k+2)` reconstruction downstream of D-pre.

## 7. Component-state inventory — PASS

A9 uses four mutually exclusive existence states:

1. `IMPLEMENTATION + SPECIFICATION`
2. `IMPLEMENTATION ONLY`
3. `SPECIFICATION ONLY`
4. `NEITHER`

Their counts must reconcile to the total component inventory.

Existence state remains distinct from clean-room usability.

## 8. Kill criteria — PASS

The assessment extracts the pre-existing reconstruction failure indicators before construction begins, including the pathological `stuck at 3` behaviour and heavy-mass drift toward 5.

These are diagnostic criteria to be recorded in advance, not post-hoc interpretations.

## 9. No new reconstruction physics output — PASS

A11 prohibits newly computed reconstruction physics output rather than numerical values generally.

New determinant/eigenvalue results, numerical h-derivatives, beta-values, beta-ratios beyond quotation of existing anchors, and k-scan outputs are prohibited. Governance measurements, inventory counts, SHAs, line numbers, timestamps, test counts, and quoted existing values remain permissible.

## 10. No premature microscopic ruling or RECON-01 design — PASS

The task does not open, close, order, rank, or make R1–R5.

It does not select a canonical kinetic operator, design the future reconstruction algorithm, construct the metric-coupled operator, or write RECON-01.

## 11. Evidential boundaries — PASS

A future successful reconstruction of `(k+2)` would not vindicate the historical Finding 5 value.

CIRC-01 remains under its existing inconclusive evidential status. Historical or recovered machinery is not silently promoted by this assessment.

## 12. Repository-bounded interpretation — PASS

The assessment is explicitly bounded by repository evidence. Absence from the repository is not converted into a claim of non-existence outside it.

This is consistent with the programme's evidence discipline.

## 13. Scope and repository integrity — PASS

The task remains restricted to its governed assessment artifacts and authorizes no modification of existing scientific derivations, scripts, gates, or historical evidence.

The expected execution scope remains four additions and zero modifications.
