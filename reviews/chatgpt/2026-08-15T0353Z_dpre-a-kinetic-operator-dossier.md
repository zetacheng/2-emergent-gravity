## Scientific review

The revised task correctly treats `D-pre-A` as a **candidate-selection dossier**, not as the canonical freeze itself. The executor is therefore required to assemble the evidence needed for a later PI choice and is not authorised to select, rank, recommend, or freeze a canonical H(4) kinetic operator.

The species-ledger requirement is now formulation-neutral. The specification no longer equates a raw Brillouin-zone zero or pole count with physical species content across all formulations. Instead, each candidate must be analysed through its own canonical free-field representation, including the appropriate corner expansion, taste reconstruction, lifted branches, or overlap-spectrum analysis. If raw zero/pole counting is used, its equivalence to physical species counting must be derived for that formulation.

The Wilson complement relation is also framed correctly. The relation `Mhat -> -8 - Mhat` is treated as arising from the global Brillouin-zone involution `p_mu -> pi - p_mu`; the association of a near-`-8` branch with the all-pi Wilson doubler sector is a Wilson-specific derived interpretation, not a premise imposed on the dossier.

The `N`-accounting requirement is appropriately conservative. The dossier must derive how each candidate's species ledger enters the programme's existing `N`-accounting before drawing any consequence for `G_c`. If that mapping is not established by the repository and the formulation analysis, the correct result is `NOT ESTABLISHED`, not an assumed multiplicative species factor.

## Governance and scope

The task correctly records the PI ruling that no a priori target species count may be used to select the canonical operator. Operator selection must later rest on independent physical and structural grounds.

The downstream question of whether some microscopic species decouple, become gapped, pair, confine, or otherwise disappear from the infrared spectrum remains a separate dynamical question. Recording that question as the authorised deferred item does not make it a criterion for choosing the microscopic theory.

The dossier does **not** itself close `OPEN-AC-4`, does not make `C-iii` evaluable, and does not authorise `D0`. A later PI selection and freeze are still required before the phase line can consume the canonical microscopic action, and the Euclidean-spectral equivalence obligation remains separately required before Route D can advance to `D0`.

## Bias control

The anti-ranking provisions are material and should be enforced semantically, not only lexically. Similar treatment depth across the candidate formulations, explicit disclosure of the executor's own preference if one arose during the work, and confirmation that no candidate was selected are appropriate safeguards for a dossier whose purpose is to support a subsequent PI ruling.

## Non-blocking clarification

For a Wilson candidate, describing the all-pi corner as a microscopic species/branch must not be broadened into a claim that it is necessarily an independent light infrared particle. The specification's lifted-versus-absent distinction is sufficient to preserve this boundary, so no amendment is required.
