**Review date:** 2026-08-19  
**Review verdict:** `APPROVE FOR EXECUTION`

## 1. Purpose and scope — PASS

The specification correctly scopes a read-only projection adjudication for the RECON line.

It asks whether the repository contains a qualifying derivation that physically justifies dropping the external complement, and separately re-assesses Components 5 and 9 under the now-landed A-EXT-01 / H-EXT-01 distinction.

It does not execute RECON-01b.

## 2. Derived versus defined/assumed — RESOLVED / PASS

The previous review identified an overly narrow derivational-ground taxonomy.

The revised specification now accepts any explicit derivation, including symmetry, Ward or gauge identities, representation theory, dynamics, exact identities, or other explicit derivational arguments.

The controlling distinction is correctly stated as:

`derived` versus `defined or assumed`.

This avoids false negatives caused by a derivation being expressed in an unanticipated formalism.

## 3. Physical-completeness criterion — PASS

A qualifying derivation must do more than define or choose the axis-TT projection.

It must support the substantive claim that the discarded complement contributes nothing relevant to the physical observable, or establish an equivalent physical-completeness statement.

This preserves the distinction between A-EXT-01 and H-EXT-01.

## 4. Q1 outcome vocabulary — RESOLVED / PASS

Q1 now has a single consistent outcome set:

- `FOUND`
- `NOT FOUND IN REPOSITORY`
- `INCONCLUSIVE`

If the question cannot be resolved by reading because a new calculation would be required, the formal Q1 outcome is:

`INCONCLUSIVE`

with reason:

`UNDETERMINED BY READING`.

This is consistent across the specification and acceptance criteria.

## 5. Computation-needed case — RESOLVED / PASS

The computation-needed condition is no longer mislabelled as an abort condition.

It is now a measurement limitation under §6.

The executor must not perform a new target-bearing or projection-completeness calculation merely to settle Q1.

This preserves the read-only nature of the task.

## 6. Abort semantics — PASS

The remaining abort conditions are genuine execution stops.

The task does not mix a permitted uncertainty state with a STOP condition.

This removes the ambiguity identified in the prior review.

## 7. A-EXT-01 dependency — PASS

The specification correctly treats A-EXT-01 as the operational definition that permits RECON-01b to use the axis-TT observable.

Q1 does not reopen that definitional ruling.

The existence or absence of a physical-completeness derivation does not invalidate the definition itself.

## 8. H-EXT-01 boundary — PASS

H-EXT-01 remains the separate physical hypothesis:

`Z_axis-TT = Z_physical`

with status `UNESTABLISHED` and explicitly not assumed by RECON-01b.

A Q1 result of `NOT FOUND IN REPOSITORY` means only that the repository currently lacks a qualifying derivation.

It does not establish H-EXT-01 as false.

## 9. RECON-01b non-blocking rule — PASS

The specification correctly prevents projection-completeness adjudication from being silently converted into a new prerequisite for the axis-TT-defined RECON-01b test.

Even an unfavourable Q1 result does not, by itself, block RECON-01b.

The result instead constrains the interpretation of what RECON-01b can establish.

## 10. Component 5 decomposition — PASS

Component 5 is correctly separated into:

1. implementation;
2. specification;
3. physical completeness.

Physical completeness is explicitly excluded from the implementation/specification classification.

Therefore Component 5 may be classified as implementation + specification even while H-EXT-01 remains unestablished.

This is the correct consequence of the A-EXT-01 / H-EXT-01 split.

## 11. Component 5 reclassification discipline — PASS

The executor must base Component 5's reclassification on repository evidence for `TT_RECIPES` implementation and specification.

The task may not downgrade the component merely because complement irrelevance has not been proven.

Nor may it upgrade physical completeness merely because the recipe is documented and implemented.

## 12. Component 9 scope question — PASS

The specification correctly asks whether Component 9's unresolved requirement concerns:

- observable specification; or
- projection physical completeness.

This distinction is essential.

If the missing requirement is observable specification, A-EXT-01 may have changed the component's readiness state.

If it is physical completeness, the issue remains under H-EXT-01.

The specification does not pre-decide which reading is correct.

## 13. Documentary evidence discipline — PASS

The task is appropriately limited to repository reading and classification.

Search hits must be read in context.

Absence of a phrase is not, by itself, evidence that a derivation is absent unless the relevant corpus and exclusions are explicitly justified.

This is especially important given the repository's prior substring hazards.

## 14. No target-bearing calculation — PASS

The specification does not authorize a β_V value, k-scan, pole calculation, discarded-space remeasurement, or other target-bearing numerical work.

It is a documentary/structural adjudication.

## 15. Epistemic interpretation — PASS

The specification preserves the model-level evidence architecture:

`axis-TT-defined reconstruction`

is distinct from

`independent proof that the full dynamics selects the spin-2 channel`.

A successful RECON-01b result may support reconstruction within the defined sector.

It cannot independently establish vanishing spin-1/0 residues if those channels were removed by definition before the reconstruction quantity was formed.

## 16. Acceptance structure — PASS

The acceptance criteria are consistent with the revised outcome vocabulary and read-only scope.

In particular, an `INCONCLUSIVE` result with reason `UNDETERMINED BY READING` is a valid completed outcome where appropriate and is not confused with an abort.

## 17. Remaining STOP-level defects

None found.

The three issues identified in the previous review are resolved:

1. the derivational-ground test now accepts any explicit derivation rather than only a narrow structural class;
2. Q1 has a consistent three-state outcome vocabulary, with `UNDETERMINED BY READING` recorded as a reason for `INCONCLUSIVE`;
3. the computation-needed condition has been removed from the abort-condition taxonomy.
