## Review scope

This review is bound to the exact uploaded specification bytes identified by the SHA-256 above.

The revised CHANNEL-B0 specification was reviewed for scientific scope, spin/channel classification, coupling-source interpretation, separation of coupling strength from universality, equivalence-principle claims, interaction with the completed but non-landed EPS-B0 scope result, evidence-base discipline, verdict taxonomy, and execution/governance constraints.

## 1. Channel question — PASS

The task is correctly scoped to determine whether the repository distinguishes the spin-2 transverse-traceless gravitational channel from the spin-0 angular/scalar channel, and to classify what is actually stated about each channel's source and coupling.

It does not infer final dark-matter versus modified-gravity ontology merely from mediator spin.

## 2. Coupling strength versus universality — PASS

The revised specification correctly separates coupling magnitude from coupling universality.

An epsilon- or mixing-suppressed interaction is not thereby non-universal. A weak coupling may still be universal if the relevant scalar charge/coupling relation is common across matter.

Accordingly, A6 may classify the manuscript evidence as `UNIVERSAL`, `NON-UNIVERSAL`, or `UNSTATED` without using suppression itself as evidence of non-universality.

## 3. Scalar mediator classification — PASS

The specification no longer treats spin-0 exchange as automatically non-universal or automatically a dark-matter mechanism.

A spin-0 mediator is classified as a scalar-mediated additional force. Whether it belongs to a dark-sector interpretation, scalar-tensor modified gravity, or another ontology depends on the actual coupling/source structure and on the manuscript's own claims.

## 4. EPS-B0 interaction — PASS

The specification correctly recognizes that the completed EPS-B0 result is not landed in the CHANNEL-B0 evidence base.

It therefore does **not** instruct the executor to cite or independently verify EPS-B0 as repository evidence.

Instead, EPS-B0 is carried only as a specification-level reporting constraint.

This is the correct treatment for evidence-base integrity.

## 5. Epsilon-dependent magnitude rider — PASS

Under the §1a reporting constraint, CHANNEL-B0 may classify the stated scalar coupling structure but must not report its microscopic magnitude as frozen or independently derived.

This does not block A6.

The task can still determine what the manuscript states about the source and universality of the coupling while keeping the numerical strength epistemically separate.

The important distinction is:

`universality != coupling strength != numerical closure`.

## 6. Channel independence versus parameter independence — PASS

The specification correctly requires the report to preserve the distinction:

**Channel separation does not establish parameter independence.**

A finding that the spin-0 and spin-2 channels are structurally distinct does not imply that every parameter controlling the scalar channel is independently fixed.

This rider prevents `CHANNELS SEPARATED` from being over-interpreted as a stronger microscopic-independence result.

## 7. Evidence-base discipline — PASS

The revised specification explicitly records that EPS-B0 is non-landed relative to the declared evidence base and therefore cannot be treated as citable evidence in this task.

This is preferable to adding the EPS-B0 branch artifact as a required evidence source before integration.

Repository claims made by CHANNEL-B0 must remain grounded in material actually present at the declared evidence base.

## 8. Kinetic coefficient versus source coupling — PASS

The specification correctly preserves the limitation that a kinetic coefficient such as Z does not, by itself, establish the matter source, scalar charge, or coupling law.

This prevents the existence of an induced gravitational kinetic term from being misread as completion of the source side.

## 9. Equivalence-principle classification — PASS

The specification appropriately distinguishes among an equivalence-principle statement that is merely claimed, derived in the repository, derived elsewhere and cited, or tested.

This prevents qualitative manuscript language from being upgraded into a demonstrated repository result.

## 10. Verdict taxonomy — PASS

The channel-level verdicts remain appropriately bounded:

- `CHANNELS SEPARATED`
- `CHANNELS CONFLATED`
- `SEPARATION NOT STATED`

These verdicts concern the channel structure actually established by the repository. They do not independently adjudicate dark-matter ontology, modified-gravity ontology, coupling magnitude, or microscopic parameter closure.

## 11. No new physics computation — PASS

The task remains a read-only scientific classification/scope assessment.

It does not calculate a new coupling, infer epsilon, derive a new fifth-force strength, or introduce a new microscopic interaction.

This is consistent with the evidence required to answer CHANNEL-B0.

## 12. Propagation of the revisions — PASS

The revised treatment is not confined to introductory prose.

The strength/universality distinction and the EPS-B0 reporting rider propagate through the relevant acceptance criteria, channel verdict interpretation, Rule 16 junctions, report requirements, and pre-issue record.

Accordingly, the executor is not simultaneously given a neutral classification task and a contradictory pre-decided conclusion.

However, §1a and the surrounding execution contract already make the provenance explicit. This wording point is therefore **not an execution blocker**.
